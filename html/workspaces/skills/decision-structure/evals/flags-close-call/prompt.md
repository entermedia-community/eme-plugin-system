Help me decide using my values. Should I take the job offer in Denver (A) or stay at my current job in Austin (B)?

My values:
1. Family close — it's better to live within an hour of my parents than not.
2. Learn from people better than me — always work where the team is stronger than you.
3. Own the outcome — I'd rather have a small piece I fully own than a big piece I share.
4. Save before you spend — financial runway beats a nicer lifestyle.
5. Fresh air — do things outside whenever possible.
6. Slow decisions on big things — never rush a major commitment.
7. Mentors matter — a good boss is worth more than a good title.
8. Simple living — fewer possessions, smaller place.
9. Say it face to face — hard conversations are better in person.
10. Build things that last — prefer long-term craft over quick wins.

A: Denver. Senior engineer at a strong 400-person company, 15% raise, team is very experienced, great manager from my references. Mountains nearby. 14 hours' drive from my parents. Would have to sell my house, which costs about two months of savings in moving costs.
B: Austin. Current job, a 12-person startup where I'm the only senior engineer and own the whole backend. Parents are 40 minutes away. Boss is okay but not a mentor. Hot summers, mostly indoors. No raise this year but stable.
